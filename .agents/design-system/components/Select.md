# Select

A choice from a short, fixed list: a plan, a payment method, a room.

- Use the native `<select>`, styled to match TextInput. The phone's own picker is faster and lighter than a custom menu on low-end Android.
- Chevron-down icon on the right, `on-surface-variant`, `icon-medium`.
- Show the price in plan options when the choice costs money: "Monthly hot desk · ₦85,000".
- More than about 12 options needs a search field instead.
- States follow TextInput.
